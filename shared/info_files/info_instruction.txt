You can copy and paste your resume. However read all the questions. Then delete the questions covered in the resume.  

Personal Information & Contact:

* What is your full name and preferred contact information? (Includes phone, email, LinkedIn/portfolio link):
* What is your current location or preferred work location?

Summary/Objective:

* In 2-3 sentences, summarize your key skills and career goals. (Tests ability to concisely explain themselves)
* What specific type of role or industry are you targeting in your job search? (Shows focus)

Education:

* List your educational degrees, institutions, majors, and graduation dates. (Verifies credentials)
* If relevant, mention any specific academic achievements or honors. (Highlights accomplishments)

Relevant Courses/Training:

* What specific courses or training programs have you completed that are relevant to your target role? (Shows relevant skill-building)
* Have you acquired any certifications or licenses relevant to your field? (Demonstrates specialized expertise)

Skills:

* List your technical skills, including software, tools, languages, etc. (Assesses core competencies)
* What are your soft skills, such as communication, teamwork, problem-solving, etc.? (Evaluates interpersonal and work-related abilities)

Career Goal/Objective (if not covered in Summary):

* Describe your long-term career aspirations and how your current job search fits into that. (Assesses ambition and career planning)

Project Experience/Portfolio:

* Describe a project you worked on where you took a leadership role. (Highlights leadership experience)
* Detail a project where you faced a significant challenge and how you overcame it. (Demonstrates problem-solving)
* Showcase a project that you're particularly proud of and explain why. (Highlights passion and achievements)
* For each project, list the technologies/tools used, your role, and the project's duration. (Provides context and specific skills used)

Experience:

* Describe your work experience, including company name, job title, dates, and responsibilities. (Verifies work history)
* Provide specific examples of accomplishments and quantifiable results in your previous roles. (Demonstrates impact)
* What were your key learnings or takeaways from each role? (Shows reflection and growth)

References:

* Do you have references available upon request? Provide contact information for your references (If requested, ensures proper permissions)

** Adaptable Questions, We will determine your mindset and it can be helpful when writing the cover letter.

* Would you like to share your strengths and weaknesses?
* Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
* Any personal philosophy of professional life you would like to share?
