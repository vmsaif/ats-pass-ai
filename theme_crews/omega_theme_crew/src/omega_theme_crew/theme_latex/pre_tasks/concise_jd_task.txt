**Job Summary for New Business Development Specialist at GroupHEALTH Benefit Solutions**

**Essential Responsibilities:**

* Identify potential clients across Canada using various tools and strategies
* Initiate contact with prospective clients through phone and email to introduce GroupHEALTH's offerings
* Assess client needs and readiness to determine their suitability for GroupHEALTH's services
* Schedule appointments between qualified prospects and licensed advisors
* Maintain organized records of client interactions and track progress
* Collaborate with sales professionals to share insights and best practices
* Meet or exceed individual and team goals for lead generation and appointments set
* Represent GroupHEALTH with professionalism and uphold the company's values in all interactions

**Essential Qualifications:**

* Experience in cold-calling, prospecting, or business development
* Strong communication and interpersonal skills
* Self-motivated and driven to achieve goals
* Ability to think quickly and adapt to changing situations
* Eagerness to learn and take on new challenges
* Proficiency in modern sales tools

**Additional Perks and Benefits:**

* Competitive salary and benefits package
* Uncapped commission structure with potential for high earnings
* Hybrid work schedule (2 days in office, 3 days at home)
* Flexible schedule and a predictable work schedule to maintain work-life balance
* Health and wellness benefits, including dental care, extended health care, and wellness programs
* Professional development opportunities and career growth potential
